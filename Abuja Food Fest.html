Home / About

Our Story

The Culture Behind the Cuisine

Abuja Food Fest started as a small gathering of food lovers and has grown into the capital's most anticipated food and lifestyle experience.

Who we are

Built by food lovers, for the whole city

Abuja Food Fest is a food, culture and entertainment platform bringing together the best local vendors, emerging brands and live performers under one roof. We started in 2023 with a single campus pop-up and have since grown into a multi-city, multi-campus movement.

Every event we run is designed around one idea: food tastes better shared. Whether it's a university courtyard or Jabi Lake's open grounds, we build spaces where people eat well, connect, and discover something new.

Our Mission

To create accessible, high-energy spaces where Nigerian food culture, small businesses and entertainment thrive together.

Our Vision

To become West Africa's leading food and lifestyle festival platform, known for discovering the next generation of food brands.

What We Represent

Community, culture and celebration — a stage for Nigerian vendors, chefs and performers to be seen and supported.

Campus Tour

Abuja Food Fest — Campus Tour 2026

5 campuses · Free entry · Vendors from across Abuja

About This Event

The Campus Tour brings Abuja Food Fest directly to students — five universities, five food-filled days. Local vendors set up shop between lectures, turning ordinary campus grounds into a festival of flavour, music and community. It's our way of reaching the next generation of food lovers where they already are.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Campus Tour free to attend?

Yes — entry is completely free for students and guests at every campus stop.

Do I need a ticket?

No ticket is required for the Campus Tour. Just show up at any of the five campus dates.

How can my business sell at a stop?

Vendors can book a stall through our Vendor Registration page — packages start at ₦20,000 per stall.

Can my school host a future stop?

Yes — reach out via our Contact page and select "Partnership Enquiry" to discuss hosting.
